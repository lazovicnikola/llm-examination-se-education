Database Search Strategy

1. ACM Digital Library

Search Scope:

* Title
* Abstract
* Last 5 years

Search String:
(LLM* OR "large language model*" OR "generative AI" OR genAI) AND ("software engineering" OR "computer science") AND (education OR learning OR assessment OR exam*) AND student*

Results:

* 280 studies

---

2. IEEE Xplore

Search Scope:

* Abstract
* Document Title

Search Strings:

Abstract Search:
(("Abstract":LLM) OR ("Abstract":"large language model") OR ("Abstract":"generative ai") OR ("Abstract":"genAI"))
AND
(("Abstract":"software engineering") OR ("Abstract":"computer science"))
AND
(("Abstract":"education") OR ("Abstract":"learning") OR ("Abstract":"assessment") OR ("Abstract":"exam"))
AND
("Abstract":student)


Document Title Search:
(("Document Title":LLM) OR ("Document Title":"large language model") OR ("Document Title":"generative ai") OR ("Document Title":"genAI"))
AND
(("Document Title":"software engineering") OR ("Document Title":"computer science"))
AND
(("Document Title":"education") OR ("Document Title":"learning") OR ("Document Title":"assessment") OR ("Document Title":"exam"))
AND
("Document Title":student)

Results:

* 164 studies

---

3. Scopus

Search Scope:

* Title and Abstract

Search String:
TITLE-ABS(("large language model*" OR LLM* OR "generative AI" OR genAI) AND "software engineering" AND student* AND (education OR learning OR assessment OR exam*))
AND PUBYEAR > 2021

Results:

* Initial search: 130 studies
* Refined search with higher education/university filters: 31 studies

---

4. Web of Science

Search Scope:

* Abstract

Search String:
AB=(("large language model*" OR LLM* OR "generative AI" OR "genAI") AND ("software engineering" OR "computer science") AND student* AND (education OR assessment OR exam*))

Results:

* 195 studies
